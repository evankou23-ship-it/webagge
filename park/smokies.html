#7 Great Smoky Mountains, TN

It might be a little unfair to rank this so low, but it all fairness it's kinda just mountains. According to the NPS it's the most visited national park, but that said, when I visited it was not even smoky at all...

There, I did not have much time to hike around, but we still got some pretty breathtaking views and great photo opportunities.


We all decided to wear polo shirts headed up this mountain for some reason. This was also my first time really being INSIDE a mountain range, so safe to say it was still very cool. Only thing is, it's overshadowed by the other parks on this list.




I rate this park a 4pm on do-nothing Saturday.
